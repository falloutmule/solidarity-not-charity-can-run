# Runtime asset gallery decisions

## Verified local candidate scope

- Query `?assetgallery=1` starts `asset-gallery-v1` through the normal SNC simulation and renderer.
- The mode is `harnessOnly`, does not clear or save campaign state, and suppresses timer, pickup, delivery, exit, and progression consequences while retaining MOVE, LOOK, collision, and normal sprite rendering.
- Seven transparent candidate assets are embedded once in `SNC_RUNTIME_ASSET_REGISTRY`; level placements reference their `assetId` only.
- The original JPEG concept sheets remain byte-for-byte source references.

## Source-preserving isolation decision

- Seven separable volunteer/civilian/household subjects are candidate cutouts.
- Nine street-sheet subjects remain `blocked`: their scenery, shadows, carts, bicycles, leashes, or fine details cannot be isolated cleanly without redraw, inpainting, or invented pixels.
- Candidate cutouts are generated by `tools/character-isolation.py`, which only crops, detects border-connected background, masks it transparent, and resizes the result. It does not generate or repair artwork.

## Deferred dependency

The real `.4`, `.5`, and `.6` obstruction bays are not represented by fake geometry. They remain deferred until the independent low-block raycaster spike is accepted. The gallery level records this dependency explicitly.

## Local-only status

This branch is an unmerged, unpublished runtime candidate. It has no PR, deployment, or public preview authorization.
