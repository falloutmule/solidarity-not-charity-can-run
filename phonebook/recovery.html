Details & sources

Bed count

60 beds at the Larimer Street facility in a November 14, 2023 report republished by Step. The report also gives up to 90 places including six houses; 90 is not the size of the entry facility. Current capacity is not confirmed.

Cost and stay

After employment, Step lists $15/day, $75/week, or $200/month in program fees, plus buying and preparing food. Confirm the applicable payment option. No fixed stay length.

Admission

Applications normally Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–noon, for same-day intake if accepted. Out-of-state exceptions are possible. Full-time employment and program participation are required. Not a clinical detox facility; ask about all prescriptions.

Bed count

Colorado’s housing division lists 192 recovery-housing beds (December 22, 2025). This is published capacity, not verified staffed beds or vacancies. AllHealth Network’s live page separately lists 27 withdrawal-management beds; those clinical beds are not added to the housing count or included in this listing’s cost statement.

Cost evidence

January 6, 2026 reporting quotes the operator describing Sage Ridge and Fort Lyon as free to guests. Confirm the financial terms for housing and any separate clinical service.

Admission and stay

The current operator page requires Colorado residency or Colorado Medicaid; it does not state the six-month rule reported in older coverage. Up to two years, voluntary participation, medical stability and legal screening.

Medication and daily life

The operator allows medication-assisted treatment. Methadone transfer must be arranged before intake. A 30–45-day adjustment period limits off-campus activity; outside employment begins no earlier than six months. Read current rules with the referral team.

Details & sources

Bed count

The live operator FAQ lists 225 residential recovery beds. A CARITAS presentation in Virginia’s 2023 Abatement Academy lists 196 men’s + 142 women’s beds = 338 on slide 16. Its separate 36 men’s + 28 women’s emergency-shelter beds (64) are excluded. The sources do not explain the difference. This guide keeps the FAQ’s 225 as its sorting figure, not a verified current inventory; confirm both campus counts and available openings directly.

Cost and access

The Healing Place recovery program is free and does not bill private insurance or Medicaid. This is separate from emergency-shelter admission. Ask about any later transitional-housing costs.

Stay and healthcare

The education component commonly takes 6–8 months, followed by a six-week workforce program. On-site medical, behavioral-health and opioid-use-disorder medication services are described. Confirm the exact medication and intake requirements before travel.

Details & sources

Bed count

The operator describes its San Francisco complex as housing for 500. This is design capacity, not a current staffed-bed inventory. Other campuses are not included in the count.

Cost and commitment

Minimum two years. Internal work is unpaid. During the final three months before graduation, residents work outside: half of each paycheck is saved for them and half covers room, board and related costs. The stay is not free throughout every phase.

Medical fit

The operator describes residents as ages 18–68, not a strict age cutoff. It excludes dual-diagnosis applicants and people requiring psychiatric medication, and does not offer professional counseling. Do not stop prescribed care to qualify; discuss an appropriate placement with your prescriber.

What this shorter guide includes

A focused shortlist, not a complete inventory: Colorado facilities plus programs from the earlier guide with explicit nationwide admission, followed by two national no-charge service networks. Colorado programs also open to out-of-state applicants are not duplicated.

Programs elsewhere with local-only or unverified nationwide eligibility, the 50-state directory, rent-only housing, and funding-only entries are omitted from this edition. “National” does not mean all applicants qualify.

The numeric order uses the specific published figure shown—not a verified ranking of current operating capacity. A campus figure, a combined program figure and a design figure are not identical measures. Where sources conflict, both are described and the sorting figure is identified. Unknown counts are not zero. Annual client totals, bed-nights and planned future expansion are not treated as current beds.

Most sources are operator or government pages. Dated journalism is identified where it supplies a missing capacity or cost statement. Bed availability, individual acceptance and personalized charges were not verified by phone. Source links open online; the guide itself works offline.

Updated and source-checked: November 17, 2026. This directory is not a clinical placement assessment or an endorsement. Historical source dates remain visible; a new review date does not make an older bed count current. No live vacancies or individual acceptance were confirmed.
