Details & sources

Bed count

The organization's ColoradoGives profile lists 65 beds at the primary facility and room for up to 95 men including sober homes. An operator-published July 2025 article also describes the 65-bed Denver facility. This replaces the earlier 60-bed figure from 2023. The 95 total is not the size of the entry facility; neither figure is a vacancy count.

Cost and stay

No entry charge is published. After employment, Step lists $15/day, $75/week, or $200/month in program fees, plus buying and preparing food. Confirm the applicable payment option. There is no fixed stay length.

Admission and work

Applications normally run Monday-Friday, 7 a.m.-noon. Out-of-state intake exceptions are possible. Applicants must be sober at intake and able to work a full-time payroll job. The operator lists restrictions on outside financial support and government stipends. This is not a clinical detox service; confirm all medications and screening rules.

Details & sources

Bed count

The operator's March 2026 fact sheet lists 192 recovery-housing places at full capacity. AllHealth Network separately lists 27 withdrawal-management beds on campus. Those clinical beds are excluded from this housing count and cost statement. Capacity does not establish current openings or staffed beds.

Residency: confirmation needed

The current operator webpage says Colorado residency or Colorado Medicaid. Its linked application, revised August 31, 2026, asks applicants to attest to six months living, renting, or attending school in Colorado; it does not show a Medicaid exception. The sources do not resolve which rule governs an individual application. Ask intake before arranging travel.

Cost and admission

January 2026 reporting quotes a Coalition spokesperson describing Sage Ridge and Fort Lyon as free to guests. Confirm housing terms, benefit contributions and separate medical charges. Adults must be experiencing homelessness or housing instability, voluntarily pursuing recovery and medically stable for independent daily living. Legal and medical screening applies.

Medication and daily life

The operator allows medication-assisted treatment. Methadone transfer must be arranged before intake. Stays can last up to two years. A 30-45-day initial adjustment period limits off-campus activity; outside employment begins no earlier than six months. Discuss the current rules and your prescriptions with the referral team.

Stay and medical services

The education component commonly takes 6-8 months, followed by a six-week workforce component; the whole stay can differ. The program advertises access to on-site medical, behavioral-health and opioid-use-disorder medication services. Confirm the exact prescription and intake needs before travel.

What this shorter guide includes

A focused shortlist, not a complete inventory: Colorado facilities plus programs from the earlier guide with explicit nationwide admission, followed by two national networks with generally no-charge services and stated cost exceptions. Colorado programs also open to out-of-state applicants are not duplicated.

Programs elsewhere with local-only or unverified nationwide eligibility, the 50-state directory, rent-only housing, and funding-only entries are omitted from this edition. “National” does not mean all applicants qualify.

Numeric ordering uses the particular published figure shown; it is not a ranking of verified current operating capacity. Primary-facility, combined-program and design figures measure different things. CARITAS uses its current FAQ figure with the unresolved conflict visible. TOSA and TROSA move to unknown because historical figures do not establish their current size. Unknown does not mean zero. Annual clients, daily service populations and planned expansions are excluded.

Sources are primarily operator, government or organization profiles. Dated journalism is labeled where it supplies a cost statement; a primary funder report documents an expansion. Bed availability, individual acceptance and personalized charges were not verified by phone. Source links open online; the guide itself works offline.

Web-source review: November 19, 2026. This directory is not a clinical placement assessment or an endorsement. Historical source dates remain visible; a new review date does not make an older bed count current. No live vacancies or individual acceptance were confirmed.
